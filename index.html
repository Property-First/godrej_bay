ABOUT THE PROJECT

Godrej Bandra Bay, Bandra West

Located in the prestigious neighbourhood of Bandra West, Godrej Bandra Bay presents an exclusive collection of 4 and 5 BHK luxury sea-facing residences designed for an elevated waterfront lifestyle. With breathtaking views of the Arabian Sea and the iconic Bandra-Worli Sea Link, the development offers a distinctive blend of contemporary architecture, refined interiors, and thoughtfully curated lifestyle amenities. Spread across approximately 2.75 acres, the project features three wings with private lobbies, offering an exclusive living experience in one of Mumbai's most sought-after locations. With convenient connectivity to the Bandra-Worli Sea Link, Bandra-Kurla Complex (BKC), leading hospitals, premium schools, and lifestyle destinations, Godrej Bandra Bay brings together waterfront tranquillity and the convenience of urban living.
